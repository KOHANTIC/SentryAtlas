Accessibility checks

  1. Text contrast 4.5:1, marks and rules 3:1In both themes, against the ground each sits on. The token file (#31) carries a test that computes every pair.
  2. Targets at least 24 by 24 pxEvery control; the sheet's buttons and the map's markers when tapped. Aim for 44 px on phones.
  3. No drag-only controlThe sheet steps by its More, Less and Map buttons; a time slider (#42) has buttons or a field beside it.
  4. Focus order follows the pageSkip link, header, key and filters, the list, then the open event; opening an event moves focus to its title, closing returns it to the row.
  5. Focus is always visibleA 3 px ring in the action color, offset from the control, in both themes.
  6. Severity never by color aloneFill and the printed word, as round 1 drew it.
  7. Reduced motion respectedWith it set, the map jumps instead of flying, and the sheet moves without easing.
  8. The list is the map's twinEverything on the map is reachable as text, with the same filters (#37).
